🏆 First place, Best Diploma Project, AI Day

Data Guidance Navigator

Data Guidance Navigator is a sales analytics platform for people who aren't data experts. They upload an Excel file, and it explains the numbers and predicts future sales.

We presented it at AI Day and won first place for Best Diploma Project.

  • Reads your Excel sales files and cleans them for you
  • Shows total sales, order counts and average order value
  • Forecasts future sales with a Linear Regression model
  • Warns you when stock is running low
  • Works in Arabic and English, with a dark mode
  • Signs you in securely with a one-time code sent by email
